# GAME RULES — Archipelago (Isleforge base game)

*Original rules text for the Isleforge base game. 3–4 players (engine-validated
in Milestone 1; the board and rules extend to 5–8 via later expansions).*

## Objective

Be the first to **10 victory points**.

## The board

19 hexagonal tiles in a radius-2 layout: 4 Forest, 3 Hills, 4 Fields,
4 Pasture, 3 Mountains, 1 Desert. Each non-desert tile carries a number token
(2–12 except 7). Nine harbor ports ring the coast: four 3:1 ports and five
2:1 ports (one per resource).

| Tile | Produces |
|---|---|
| Forest | Wood |
| Hills | Brick |
| Fields | Grain |
| Pasture | Wool |
| Mountains | Ore |
| Desert | Nothing (the Raider starts here) |

## Setup (snake draft)

Players place in order P1→P4 then P4→P1: one settlement on any empty corner
(not adjacent to another building), then one road touching it. When placing
the **second** settlement, collect 1 resource from each adjacent tile.

## Turn structure

1. **Roll** two dice.
   - **7:** every player holding more than 7 resource cards discards half
     (rounded down), then the roller moves the Raider (below).
   - **Other:** each tile with that number produces 1 resource per adjacent
     settlement, 2 per adjacent city — unless the Raider is on it. If the bank
     cannot cover everyone's share of a resource, **nobody** receives that
     resource this roll.
2. **Play:** in any order — build, trade, buy/play development cards.
3. **End turn.**

## Building

| Build | Cost | Rules |
|---|---|---|
| Road | 1 wood + 1 brick | On a free edge connected to your network |
| Settlement | 1 wood + 1 brick + 1 grain + 1 wool | On a free corner adjacent to your road, not adjacent to any building |
| City | 2 grain + 3 ore | Upgrades one of your settlements (now worth 2 VP, produces double) |

## The Raider

On a 7 (or via a Guardian card), move the Raider to any other tile. It blocks
that tile's production. Then steal 1 random resource from one opponent with a
building adjacent to the new tile (nothing, if they hold nothing).

## Development cards

Cost: 1 grain + 1 wool + 1 ore. Max one played per turn; a card bought this
turn cannot be played until your next turn.

| Card | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Guardian** (×14) | Move the Raider and steal, as on a 7. 3+ played earns Largest Army |
| **Trailblazer** (×2) | Place 2 roads for free (must place before ending turn, if able) |
| **Harvest** (×2) | Take any 2 resources from the bank |
| **Embargo** (×2) | Take **all** of one resource from every opponent |
| **Landmark** (×5) | Worth 1 VP, kept hidden until it wins you the game — never played |

## Trading

- **Bank:** 4:1 default; 3:1 with a generic port settlement; 2:1 for the port's
  resource with a matching port settlement.
- **Players:** on your turn, propose any offer/request; the recipient accepts
  (resources swap) or declines. Stale offers auto-decline. Pending offers clear
  at end of turn.

## Victory points

Settlements 1, cities 2, **Longest Road** 2 (5+ road segments; ties keep the
holder; opponents' buildings break the road), **Largest Army** 2 (3+ Guardians;
ties keep the holder), Landmark cards 1 each (hidden). First to 10 wins
immediately — even mid-turn.

## Resignation

A player may resign anytime. If one player remains, they win. Buildings of
resigned players stay on the board (they still block).
